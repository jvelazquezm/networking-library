**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [Fundamentals](./index.md)

## Overview

Why networks are built in layers, how the seven-layer OSI model compares with the four-layer TCP/IP model, and how encapsulation works in practice.

## Key concepts

- Layering and separation of concerns
- Encapsulation and PDUs (frame, packet, segment)
- OSI vs TCP/IP mapping
- Control, data and management planes
