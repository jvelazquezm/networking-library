# Interfaces, cabling and PoE

**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [Network Fundamentals](./index.md)

## Overview

The physical layer sets the hard limits of every network: distance, speed and reliability. Twisted-pair copper dominates access, single-mode and multi-mode fibre dominate everything that is long or fast, and pluggable transceivers (SFP, QSFP) let one switch port speak many media. Power over Ethernet lets the same cable power phones, access points and cameras.

## Key concepts

- UTP categories and RJ45
- Straight-through vs crossover and Auto-MDIX
- Single-mode vs multi-mode fibre
- Transceivers: SFP, SFP+, QSFP28, QSFP-DD
- Speed, duplex and autonegotiation
- Power over Ethernet (802.3af/at/bt)
