# Network types and topologies

**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [Network Fundamentals](./index.md)

## Overview

Networks are classified by scale (PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN), by topology (star, mesh, ring, bus, hybrid) and by the way applications interact (client-server or peer-to-peer). Modern designs mix them: a campus is a star of stars, a backbone is a partial mesh and a data centre is a Clos fabric. Knowing the vocabulary makes every design discussion easier.

## Key concepts

- PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN, SAN
- Star, mesh, ring, bus and hybrid topologies
- Physical vs logical topology
- Client-server vs peer-to-peer
- Two-tier, three-tier and spine-leaf designs (overview)
- SOHO vs enterprise vs service provider networks
