# WLAN design, site surveys and roaming

**Level:** Intermediate · **Section:** [Wireless and IoT](./index.md)

## Overview

Good Wi-Fi is designed, not installed. Predictive and on-site surveys determine AP placement; channel and power planning minimise co-channel interference; capacity planning accounts for client counts and applications. Roaming depends on the client, helped by 802.11k/v/r. High-density venues, warehouses and outdoor links each need their own approach.

## Key concepts

- Coverage vs capacity design
- Predictive, passive and active surveys
- Channel reuse and transmit power
- Band steering
- Fast roaming: 802.11k, v and r
- High-density and outdoor design
