# Wireless fundamentals and RF

**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [Wireless and IoT](./index.md)

## Overview

Wireless networks share an unpredictable medium. Signals weaken with distance and obstacles, reflect and interfere, and every station on a channel must take turns. Understanding frequency bands, channel widths, signal-to-noise ratio and CSMA/CA explains why Wi-Fi behaves as it does and why more access points is not always better.

## Key concepts

- Frequency, wavelength and bands (2.4, 5, 6 GHz)
- Channels and channel width
- dBm, RSSI, SNR and noise floor
- Antennas, gain and polarisation
- Reflection, absorption, multipath
- CSMA/CA, hidden nodes and RTS/CTS
