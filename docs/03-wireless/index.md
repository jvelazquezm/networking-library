# 03 · Wireless and IoT

Wireless LANs from the radio up — how RF behaves, how the 802.11 family evolved, how enterprise WLANs are architected, secured and designed — plus the short- and long-range technologies that connect the Internet of Things. Cellular networks have their own section (Mobile Networks).

## Topics in this section

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wireless fundamentals and RF](./wireless-fundamentals.md) | Beginner | How radio works for networking: frequency, channels, power, antennas, interference and medium access. |
| [Wi-Fi standards: from 802.11a to Wi-Fi 7](./wifi-standards.md) | Beginner | The 802.11 amendments and Wi-Fi generations: OFDM, MIMO, OFDMA, 6 GHz and multi-link operation. |
| [WLAN architectures](./wlan-architectures.md) | Beginner | Autonomous, controller-based (split-MAC), cloud-managed and distributed WLANs; CAPWAP and AP modes. |
| [Wireless security: WPA2, WPA3 and 802.1X](./wireless-security.md) | Beginner | Authentication and encryption on Wi-Fi: PSK, SAE, Enterprise mode with 802.1X, and common attacks. |
| [WLAN design, site surveys and roaming](./wlan-design.md) | Intermediate | Designing wireless for coverage, capacity and roaming: surveys, channel planning, power, band steering and fast roaming. |
| [IoT and short-range wireless](./iot-wireless.md) | Intermediate | Bluetooth, Zigbee, Thread and Matter, LoRaWAN and cellular IoT (NB-IoT and LTE-M): range, power and use cases. |
