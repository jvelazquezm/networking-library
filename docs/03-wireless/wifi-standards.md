# Wi-Fi standards: from 802.11a to Wi-Fi 7

**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [Wireless and IoT](./index.md)

## Overview

Each Wi-Fi generation added a technique to squeeze more out of the air: OFDM, MIMO and channel bonding (Wi-Fi 4 and 5), OFDMA and BSS colouring for dense environments (Wi-Fi 6), the 6 GHz band (Wi-Fi 6E) and multi-link operation with 320 MHz channels (Wi-Fi 7). Knowing what each feature actually does helps you separate real gains from marketing.

## Key concepts

- 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax/be
- Wi-Fi 4, 5, 6, 6E and 7 naming
- MIMO, MU-MIMO and spatial streams
- OFDMA and target wake time
- 6 GHz and AFC
- Multi-link operation (MLO)
