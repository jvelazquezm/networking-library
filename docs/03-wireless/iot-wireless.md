# IoT and short-range wireless

**Level:** Intermediate · **Section:** [Wireless and IoT](./index.md)

## Overview

The Internet of Things uses radios optimised for low power and low cost rather than speed. Bluetooth Low Energy connects wearables and sensors; Zigbee and Thread build low-power mesh networks in buildings, with Matter as the common application layer; LoRaWAN covers kilometres with tiny data rates; NB-IoT and LTE-M bring IoT onto cellular networks. Picking the right one is a trade-off between range, power, bandwidth and cost.

## Key concepts

- Bluetooth Classic and Bluetooth Low Energy
- IEEE 802.15.4, Zigbee and Thread
- Matter
- LoRa and LoRaWAN
- NB-IoT and LTE-M
- Range vs power vs data rate
