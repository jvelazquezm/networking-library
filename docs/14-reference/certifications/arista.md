# Arista

Arista's ACE programme has seven cumulative levels defined by job role. From level 2 upwards the exams are practical labs, and levels 6 and 7 are expert-level board and lab exams. Arista also provides free [cEOS-lab and vEOS-lab images](https://www.arista.com/en/support/software-download) for practice.

| Level | Role | Main library areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACE L1 | New to networking | Sections [01 Fundamentals](../../01-fundamentals/index.md) and [02 Switching](../../02-switching/index.md); [Routing fundamentals and route selection](../../04-routing/routing-fundamentals.md) |
| ACE L2–L3 | Early to mid-career engineer | [VLANs, trunking and inter-VLAN routing](../../02-switching/vlans.md), [Spanning Tree: STP, RSTP and Rapid PVST+](../../02-switching/spanning-tree.md), [MLAG, vPC and EVPN multihoming](../../02-switching/mlag-and-multihoming.md), [OSPF](../../04-routing/ospf.md), [BGP fundamentals](../../04-routing/bgp-fundamentals.md), [Data center fabrics: leaf-spine and BGP in the DC](../../09-data-center-and-cloud/dc-fabrics.md) |
| ACE L4 | Senior engineer | [VXLAN, Geneve and EVPN](../../09-data-center-and-cloud/vxlan-evpn.md), [Advanced BGP](../../04-routing/bgp-advanced.md), [IP multicast: IGMP, PIM, SSM, MSDP and BIER](../../04-routing/multicast.md), [Quality of Service (QoS)](../../05-ip-services/qos.md), [Monitoring: SNMP, syslog, NetFlow, IPFIX and sFlow](../../12-operations/monitoring.md) |
| ACE L5 | Network automation lead | Section [13 Automation](../../13-automation/index.md), especially [Python for network engineers](../../13-automation/python-for-networks.md), [Configuration management: Ansible, Terraform, Puppet and Chef](../../13-automation/configuration-management.md) and [Model-driven telemetry and operations: gNMI, gNOI and gRPC](../../13-automation/model-driven-telemetry.md) |
| ACE L6–L7 | Architect / expert | [Network design and high availability](../../12-operations/network-design.md), [AI data center networking: RoCEv2, InfiniBand and Ultra Ethernet](../../09-data-center-and-cloud/ai-networking.md), [Segment Routing: SR-MPLS](../../07-service-provider/segment-routing.md), plus everything above |

Official information: [Arista training and certification](https://www.arista.com/en/support/training).

:::note
Arista describes levels by role rather than by a public topic-by-topic blueprint. Use this map as orientation and confirm the scope of each level with Arista.
:::
