# Books

Free and open books come first in every area. Many paid titles are also available through subscription libraries such as O'Reilly Learning, which employers and universities often provide. Each topic page also lists the books most relevant to it.

:::info Editions matter
Prefer the latest edition, especially for certification guides and anything about 5G, cloud native or automation.
:::
