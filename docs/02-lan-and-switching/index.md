# 02 · LAN and Switching

How local networks are built: Ethernet framing and switching, segmenting with VLANs, keeping Layer 2 loop-free and extending the LAN over the air with Wi-Fi.

## Topics in this section

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ethernet and switching](./ethernet.md) | Beginner | Ethernet framing, MAC learning and forwarding, and how modern switches handle traffic at line rate. |
| [VLANs and trunking](./vlans.md) | Beginner | Segmenting one physical network into many logical ones, tagging frames and routing between VLANs. |
| [Spanning tree and loop prevention](./spanning-tree.md) | Intermediate | Why Layer 2 loops are catastrophic and how STP, RSTP and MSTP prevent them. |
| [Link aggregation (LACP)](./link-aggregation.md) | Intermediate | Bundling several physical links into one logical link for capacity and resilience, including multi-chassis designs. |
| [Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11)](./wifi.md) | Intermediate | How wireless LANs work: radio fundamentals, channel access, the evolution from 802.11a to Wi-Fi 7, and enterprise design. |
| [Port security and network access control](./port-security-and-nac.md) | Intermediate | Controlling who can plug into the network: 802.1X, MAC authentication and common Layer 2 attacks and defences. |
