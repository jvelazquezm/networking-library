# 08 · Mobile Networks

Everything about cellular networks, from radio fundamentals to the 5G System, open and disaggregated RAN, network slicing, private networks and the road to 6G. 3GPP specifications are the source of truth here and the release matters: always note which release a resource refers to.

## Topics in this section

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cellular fundamentals](./cellular-fundamentals.md) | Beginner | The ideas behind every mobile generation: cells and reuse, spectrum, duplexing, OFDM, MIMO, mobility. |
| [4G LTE and the EPC](./lte-and-epc.md) | Intermediate | The 4G architecture: E-UTRAN and eNodeB, the Evolved Packet Core, bearers, attach and handover. |
| [5G System architecture](./5g-system.md) | Intermediate | The end-to-end 5G System: NSA vs SA, the service-based core, PDU sessions, QoS flows and key procedures. |
| [5G NR and NG-RAN](./5g-nr-and-ng-ran.md) | Advanced | The 5G radio: numerologies, frame structure, the NR protocol stack, massive MIMO and the gNB CU/DU split. |
| [5G Core: SBA, PFCP, GTP-U and NGAP](./5g-core.md) | Advanced | The cloud-native core network: network functions, service-based interfaces, control/user plane separation and its protocols. |
| [Voice: IMS, VoLTE and VoNR](./ims-and-voice.md) | Advanced | How voice works on all-IP mobile networks: the IP Multimedia Subsystem, VoLTE, VoNR and EPS fallback. |
| [Open RAN](./open-ran.md) | Advanced | Disaggregating the radio access network: O-RAN architecture, open fronthaul, the RIC, xApps and rApps, SMO. |
| [Network slicing](./network-slicing.md) | Advanced | Multiple logical networks with different guarantees over shared infrastructure, end to end across RAN, transport and core. |
| [Private 5G and edge computing (MEC)](./private-5g-and-mec.md) | Intermediate | 5G for enterprises and industry: private and neutral-host networks, CBRS and local spectrum, and multi-access edge computing. |
| [Non-terrestrial networks (NTN)](./non-terrestrial-networks.md) | Advanced | Satellites and high-altitude platforms in 3GPP: GEO, MEO and LEO, direct-to-device and NB-IoT over satellite. |
| [6G](./6g.md) | Advanced | What comes after 5G: the IMT-2030 framework, research directions and the 3GPP timeline. |
