# Voice: IMS, VoLTE and VoNR

**Level:** Advanced · **Section:** [Mobile Networks](./index.md)

## Overview

With no circuit-switched domain left, mobile voice is SIP-based calling over the IP Multimedia Subsystem. VoLTE carries calls over 4G with dedicated bearers; VoNR does the same natively over 5G SA, with EPS fallback as an interim step. IMS also delivers SMS over IP, video calling and emergency calls.

## Key concepts

- IMS architecture: P-CSCF, I-CSCF, S-CSCF, HSS
- SIP in IMS
- VoLTE bearers and QoS
- VoNR and EPS fallback
- SRVCC
- Emergency calls
