# Non-terrestrial networks (NTN)

**Level:** Advanced · **Section:** [Mobile Networks](./index.md)

## Overview

3GPP standardised non-terrestrial networks from Release 17, letting satellites serve as part of the 5G RAN for both smartphones and IoT devices. LEO constellations bring lower latency but extreme Doppler and frequent handovers; transparent and regenerative payloads differ in where gNB functions live. Direct-to-device services are now commercial.

## Key concepts

- GEO, MEO and LEO
- Transparent vs regenerative payloads
- Doppler, delay and timing advance
- NR-NTN and IoT-NTN
- Direct-to-device
- Integration with terrestrial networks
