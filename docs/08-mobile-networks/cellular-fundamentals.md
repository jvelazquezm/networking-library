# Cellular fundamentals

**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [Mobile Networks](./index.md)

## Overview

Cellular networks reuse scarce spectrum by dividing coverage into cells, each served by a base station. Since 4G, the air interface is built on OFDM(A), MIMO and adaptive modulation; FDD and TDD split uplink and downlink in frequency or time. Mobility management keeps calls and sessions alive as users move between cells. These concepts carry from 4G to 5G and 6G.

## Key concepts

- Cells, sectors and frequency reuse
- Spectrum bands: low, mid, mmWave
- FDD vs TDD
- OFDM, OFDMA and SC-FDMA
- MIMO and beamforming
- Modulation and coding, link adaptation
- Handover and mobility
- Generations from 2G to 5G
