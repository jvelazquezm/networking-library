# Ethernet OAM: CFM and Y.1731

**Level:** Advanced · **Section:** [Switching and Ethernet](./index.md)

## Overview

When Ethernet became a carrier service it needed the fault management that SONET and SDH had always had. Connectivity Fault Management (IEEE 802.1ag, now part of 802.1Q) adds continuity checks, loopback and linktrace between maintenance endpoints; ITU-T Y.1731 adds delay, loss and jitter measurement. Link OAM (802.3ah) monitors individual links.

## Key concepts

- Maintenance domains, associations and MEPs/MIPs
- Continuity check messages
- Loopback and linktrace
- Y.1731 delay and loss measurement
- Link OAM (802.3ah)
