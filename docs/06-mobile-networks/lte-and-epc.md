# 4G LTE and the EPC

**Level:** Intermediate · **Section:** [Mobile Networks](./index.md)

## Overview

The architecture of 4G: E-UTRAN, eNodeB, the Evolved Packet Core and the bearer model that 5G evolved from.

## Key concepts

- eNodeB and E-UTRAN
- MME, SGW, PGW, HSS
- Bearers and QoS (QCI)
- Attach and handover procedures
- VoLTE and IMS
