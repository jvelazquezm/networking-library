# 10 · Network Operations

Running networks well: knowing what is happening, prioritising traffic, finding faults quickly, measuring performance and designing for growth and failure.

## Topics in this section

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Monitoring and observability](./monitoring-and-observability.md) | Intermediate | From SNMP and syslog to flow data and streaming telemetry: how to know what your network is doing. |
| [Quality of Service (QoS)](./qos.md) | Intermediate | Classifying, marking, queuing and shaping traffic so that what matters gets through when links are congested. |
| [Troubleshooting methodology](./troubleshooting.md) | Beginner | A structured way to find faults: layered approaches, the essential tools and how to read packet captures. |
| [Performance measurement](./performance-measurement.md) | Intermediate | Measuring throughput, latency, jitter and loss correctly, both actively and passively. |
| [Network design principles](./network-design.md) | Advanced | Designing networks that scale and survive failures: hierarchy, redundancy, capacity planning and documentation. |
