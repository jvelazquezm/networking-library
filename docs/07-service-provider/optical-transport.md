# Optical transport: DWDM and OTN

**Level:** Advanced · **Section:** [Service Provider and Transport Networks](./index.md)

## Overview

Underneath every backbone, DWDM puts dozens of wavelengths on each fibre pair, amplified every 80–100 km and switched by ROADMs. Coherent optics push 400G–1.6T per wavelength, and pluggable coherent modules (400ZR, 800ZR) let routers plug directly into the optical line system. OTN provides framing, multiplexing and OAM for transport services.

## Key concepts

- Fibre impairments: attenuation, dispersion, nonlinearity
- DWDM grids and flexible grid
- EDFAs and Raman amplification
- ROADMs and colourless/directionless/contentionless designs
- Coherent optics and pluggables (400ZR, 800ZR)
- OTN hierarchy
- IP over DWDM and open line systems
