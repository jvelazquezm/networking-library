# Broadband access: PON, DOCSIS, BNG, PPPoE and IPoE

**Level:** Intermediate · **Section:** [Service Provider and Transport Networks](./index.md)

## Overview

Fixed broadband reaches homes over fibre (GPON, XGS-PON, 25G/50G PON), coaxial cable (DOCSIS) or legacy copper (DSL). Behind the access network, a broadband network gateway authenticates subscribers (PPPoE or IPoE with DHCP), applies policy and assigns addresses, usually with RADIUS. Disaggregated and cloud-native BNGs are an active area of change.

## Key concepts

- PON architecture: OLT, splitters, ONT
- GPON, XGS-PON and higher-speed PON
- DOCSIS 3.1 and 4.0
- DSL (VDSL2, G.fast) and its decline
- BNG functions
- PPPoE vs IPoE
- RADIUS and subscriber policy
- CGNAT and IPv6 for subscribers
