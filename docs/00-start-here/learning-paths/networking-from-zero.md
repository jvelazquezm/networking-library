# Networking from zero

**Level:** Beginner · **Topics:** 38

From 'what is a packet?' to building, securing and troubleshooting a small enterprise network. Covers the CCNA, JNCIA and Network+ level.

## Goal

Understand how data moves across a network and be able to build, secure, automate the basics of and troubleshoot a small enterprise network.

## Steps

1. [How the Internet works](../../01-fundamentals/how-the-internet-works.md) (Beginner) — Get the big picture first.
2. [Networking devices and their roles](../../01-fundamentals/networking-devices.md) (Beginner) — Learn what each box does.
3. [OSI and TCP/IP models](../../01-fundamentals/osi-and-tcp-ip-models.md) (Beginner) — Learn the layered model you will use for everything else.
4. [Interfaces, cabling and PoE](../../01-fundamentals/interfaces-cabling-and-poe.md) (Beginner) — Understand the physical layer.
5. [Working with network devices: the CLI](../../01-fundamentals/device-cli.md) (Beginner) — Get comfortable on the CLI of two vendors.
6. [IPv4 addressing and the IPv4 header](../../01-fundamentals/ipv4-addressing.md) (Beginner) — Learn IPv4 addressing and the header.
7. [Subnetting, VLSM and supernetting](../../01-fundamentals/subnetting.md) (Beginner) — Master subnetting — practise until it is automatic.
8. [Ethernet switching and MAC learning](../../02-switching/ethernet-switching.md) (Beginner) — Learn how switches forward frames.
9. [ARP and ICMP](../../01-fundamentals/arp-and-icmp.md) (Beginner) — See how the first hop really works.
10. [Routing fundamentals and route selection](../../04-routing/routing-fundamentals.md) (Beginner) — Move packets between networks.
11. [Static routing](../../04-routing/static-routing.md) (Beginner) — Route by hand.
12. [Life of a packet](../../01-fundamentals/life-of-a-packet.md) (Beginner) — Put the layers together hop by hop.
13. [VLANs, trunking and inter-VLAN routing](../../02-switching/vlans.md) (Beginner) — Segment the LAN.
14. [Spanning Tree: STP, RSTP and Rapid PVST+](../../02-switching/spanning-tree.md) (Beginner) — Keep Layer 2 loop-free.
15. [Link aggregation: LACP and EtherChannel](../../02-switching/link-aggregation.md) (Beginner) — Bundle links.
16. [Discovery protocols: LLDP and CDP](../../02-switching/lldp-and-cdp.md) (Beginner) — Discover your neighbours.
17. [OSPF](../../04-routing/ospf.md) (Beginner) — Run your first link-state protocol.
18. [First hop redundancy: VRRP, HSRP and GLBP](../../04-routing/first-hop-redundancy.md) (Beginner) — Make the gateway redundant.
19. [TCP and UDP](../../01-fundamentals/tcp-and-udp.md) (Beginner) — Understand transport.
20. [IPv6: addressing, NDP and SLAAC](../../01-fundamentals/ipv6.md) (Beginner) — Add IPv6.
21. [DHCP and DHCPv6](../../05-ip-services/dhcp.md) (Beginner) — Automate addressing.
22. [DNS, DNSSEC, DoT and DoH](../../05-ip-services/dns.md) (Beginner) — Learn how names work.
23. [NAT and PAT](../../05-ip-services/nat.md) (Beginner) — Connect to the Internet.
24. [Time: NTP, NTS and PTP](../../05-ip-services/time-synchronization.md) (Beginner) — Synchronise time.
25. [Device management: SSH, SNMP, syslog, TFTP, FTP and SFTP](../../05-ip-services/device-management.md) (Beginner) — Manage devices securely.
26. [Quality of Service (QoS)](../../05-ip-services/qos.md) (Beginner) — Prioritise traffic.
27. [Security concepts and programs](../../11-security/security-concepts.md) (Beginner) — Learn security basics.
28. [Access control lists](../../11-security/acls.md) (Beginner) — Filter traffic.
29. [Layer 2 security](../../11-security/layer2-security.md) (Beginner) — Secure the access layer.
30. [Wireless fundamentals and RF](../../03-wireless/wireless-fundamentals.md) (Beginner) — Learn wireless basics.
31. [WLAN architectures](../../03-wireless/wlan-architectures.md) (Beginner) — Learn WLAN architectures.
32. [Wireless security: WPA2, WPA3 and 802.1X](../../03-wireless/wireless-security.md) (Beginner) — Secure the WLAN.
33. [WAN technologies](../../06-wan-and-vpn/wan-technologies.md) (Beginner) — Connect sites.
34. [Virtualization: hypervisors, VMs and virtual switching](../../09-data-center-and-cloud/virtualization.md) (Beginner) — Understand virtualisation and cloud.
35. [Network management approaches](../../13-automation/management-approaches.md) (Beginner) — See how networks are managed today.
36. [REST APIs](../../13-automation/rest-apis.md) (Beginner) — Talk to APIs.
37. [AI in network operations](../../13-automation/ai-for-networks.md) (Beginner) — Use AI responsibly in operations.
38. [Troubleshooting methodology and tools](../../12-operations/troubleshooting.md) (Beginner) — Put it all together by fixing broken networks.

## Milestone project

Build a two-site network with VLANs, OSPF, VRRP, DHCP, NAT, ACLs and Wi-Fi in a virtual lab, then document it and fix three faults someone else introduces.

:::tip How to work through a path
Read the overview and key concepts of each topic, do its hands-on lab, and move on only when you can explain every key concept without notes.
:::
