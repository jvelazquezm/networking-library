# 05 · Carrier and Transport Networks

What sits underneath the Internet and the mobile network: optical transport, fibre and cable access, metro and backhaul networks, and the timing that mobile radio depends on.

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |

| [Optical transport (DWDM and OTN)](./optical-transport.md) | Advanced | Moving terabits over fibre: wavelength multiplexing, amplification, coherent optics and the OTN framing hierarchy. |
| [Fixed access: FTTH, PON and cable](./fixed-access.md) | Intermediate | Last-mile technologies to homes and businesses: passive optical networks, DOCSIS cable and DSL. |
| [Metro, Carrier Ethernet and mobile backhaul](./metro-and-backhaul.md) | Intermediate | Connecting cell sites and enterprises: Carrier Ethernet services, fronthaul vs backhaul and transport requirements for 5G. |
