# 06 · WAN, VPNs and Overlays

How sites are connected to each other and to the cloud: the transport options available, the tunnelling and encryption technologies that run over them, and the overlay architectures — from DMVPN to SD-WAN — that turn many links into one manageable WAN.

## Topics in this section

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [WAN technologies](./wan-technologies.md) | Beginner | Leased lines, MPLS VPNs, Metro Ethernet, broadband Internet, 4G/5G and satellite as WAN transports, and WAN topologies. |
| [GRE tunnels](./gre.md) | Intermediate | Generic Routing Encapsulation: carrying any protocol, including multicast and routing protocols, over IP. |
| [IPsec and IKEv2](./ipsec.md) | Intermediate | Encrypting IP traffic: the IPsec architecture, ESP, IKEv2 negotiation, tunnel and transport mode, policy- and route-based VPNs. |
| [Remote access VPN: TLS VPN and WireGuard](./remote-access-vpn.md) | Intermediate | Connecting individual users: IPsec and TLS-based client VPNs, WireGuard, and how they compare with ZTNA. |
| [DMVPN and NHRP](./dmvpn.md) | Advanced | Dynamic multipoint VPNs: mGRE, NHRP and IPsec building on-demand spoke-to-spoke tunnels. |
| [SD-WAN](./sd-wan.md) | Intermediate | Software-defined WAN: centralised control, transport independence, application-aware routing and the main vendor architectures. |
| [LISP](./lisp.md) | Advanced | Locator/ID Separation Protocol: separating who a host is from where it is, and its role in campus fabrics. |
