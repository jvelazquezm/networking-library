# WAN technologies

**Level:** Beginner · **Section:** [WAN, VPNs and Overlays](./index.md)

## Overview

Enterprises connect sites with a mix of transports. Private services (leased lines, MPLS L3VPN, Metro Ethernet) offer predictable performance; Internet broadband, 4G/5G and low-Earth-orbit satellite offer cheap bandwidth and diversity. Topologies range from hub-and-spoke to full mesh. Understanding the trade-offs is the starting point for any WAN or SD-WAN design.

## Key concepts

- Leased lines and point-to-point links
- MPLS L3VPN as a service
- Metro Ethernet (E-Line, E-LAN)
- Broadband: DSL, cable, fibre
- 4G/5G and satellite (including LEO) as WAN links
- Hub-and-spoke, partial and full mesh
- Internet VPN and hybrid WAN
