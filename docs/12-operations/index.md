# 12 · Network Operations and Assurance

Running networks well: a method for finding faults quickly, the data sources that tell you what the network is doing, the tools to measure performance actively, and the design principles that make networks easier to operate in the first place.

## Topics in this section

| Topic | Level | What you will learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Troubleshooting methodology and tools](./troubleshooting.md) | Beginner | Structured approaches to finding faults and the essential tools: ping, traceroute, mtr, show commands, captures and logs. |
| [Monitoring: SNMP, syslog, NetFlow, IPFIX and sFlow](./monitoring.md) | Beginner | The classic monitoring data sources: polled counters, events and flow records — and what each is good for. |
| [Observability stack: Prometheus and Grafana](./observability-stack.md) | Intermediate | Modern metrics, logs and dashboards for networks: time-series databases, exporters, dashboards and alerting. |
| [Active monitoring: IP SLA, TWAMP and STAMP](./active-monitoring.md) | Intermediate | Measuring latency, jitter and loss with synthetic probes, from router-based IP SLA to standard TWAMP and STAMP. |
| [Packet capture and port mirroring](./packet-capture.md) | Intermediate | Getting packets to an analyser: SPAN, RSPAN, ERSPAN, TAPs, on-box capture and analysing captures in Wireshark. |
| [Network design and high availability](./network-design.md) | Intermediate | Designing networks that scale and survive failures: hierarchy, modularity, redundancy, failure domains, capacity planning and documentation. |
