# OSPF

**Level:** Intermediate · **Section:** [Routing and Internet Architecture](./index.md)

## Overview

The most widely deployed link-state IGP in enterprises: LSAs, areas, adjacencies and convergence.

## Key concepts

- Neighbour states and adjacencies
- LSA types and the LSDB
- Areas and ABRs
- SPF calculation
- OSPFv3 for IPv6
